# FIREpath — short video scripts (Reels / YouTube Shorts)

10 scripts for 30–40 second vertical videos, each based on one idea from a well-known money book (summarised in our own words).
Videos 1–3 are also produced automatically as MP4s by the video maker in `marketing/video/`.

**Rules for every video**
- Explain ideas in your own words. No book covers, long quotes or author photos. “Idea from *Book*” is fine; never suggest the author endorses FIREpath.
- Education only. Don’t recommend specific funds or stocks, and don’t promise returns. Show **“Illustrative · not investment advice”** whenever numbers appear.
- Number convention used below: SIP invested monthly, 12% a year (1% a month), before tax and inflation. All figures were calculated, not guessed.
- Keep important text in the middle of the screen. Instagram and YouTube cover the top ~250px and bottom ~400px with their buttons.
- End card for all: **“Find your freedom age — free. FIREpath · link in bio.”**
- Music: add a trending or royalty-free track inside Instagram or YouTube (or the YouTube Audio Library). Don’t use copyrighted songs in paid ads.

## 2. Pay yourself first (*Rich Dad Poor Dad*)
**Hook:** “Why your ‘I’ll invest what’s left’ plan never works.”
| Time | Voiceover | On screen |
|---|---|---|

| 12–22s | “Flip it. Set your SIP for the day after salary day. Then live on the rest.” | 1st: salary · **2nd: SIP** · rest: life |
| 22–30s | “Just ₹5,000 a month this way becomes about ₹50 lakh in 20 years.” | ₹5,000/month → ₹49.5 lakh (20 yrs) |
| 30–35s | CTA | End card |

**Caption:** Investing first turns saving into a default, not a decision. Set your SIP date to salary +1. 📅 #payyourselffirst #richdadpoordad #SIP #moneyhabits #FIRE

## 3. Need less, get free sooner (*Early Retirement Extreme*)
**Hook:** “It’s not your salary that decides when you retire. It’s this number.”
| Time | Voiceover | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 3–8s | “Your savings rate: the share of income you keep.” | **Savings rate = what you keep ÷ what you earn** |
| 8–24s | “Save 10%, and you’d work about 51 years. 20%: about 36. 30%: 28. 50%: just 16 years.” | Bars shrink: 10% → 51 yrs … 70% → 9 yrs |
| 24–31s | “And this works on any income. It’s not how much you earn, it’s how much you keep.” | **Any income.** |
| 31–36s | CTA: “Check your savings rate free on FIREpath.” | End card |

**Footnote on screen:** Starting from zero, 5% real return, 4% withdrawal rate. Illustrative.
**Caption:** Every 10% you save can cut a decade off your working life. What’s your savings rate? 👇 #savingsrate #FIRE #earlyretirement #financialindependence

## 4. The raise trap (*The Psychology of Money*)
**Hook:** “You got a ₹40,000 raise. Here’s how to stay broke anyway.”
- “Step 1: buy a bigger car on EMI. Step 2: there is no step 2. You’re exactly where you started.”
- “Instead: enjoy half the raise. Invest the other ₹20,000.”
- On screen: ₹20,000/month × 15 years → **≈ ₹1 crore**
- “Know what ‘enough’ is. Then let every raise buy you freedom, not just stuff.” → CTA
**Caption:** Lifestyle creep cancels raises. The 50/50 raise rule: enjoy half, invest half. #lifestylecreep #salaryhike #moneymindset

## 5. The 10% rule from ancient Babylon (*The Richest Man in Babylon*)
**Hook:** “A 100-year-old book has the simplest wealth rule ever.”
- “Keep at least one-tenth of everything you earn. Before rent. Before shopping.”
- On screen: Salary ₹60,000 → keep ₹6,000 → 25 years → **≈ ₹1.1 crore** (invested ₹18 lakh)
- “Start with 10%. Grow it every year.” → CTA
**Caption:** One-tenth of everything you earn is yours to keep. Start with 10%, then raise it. #richestmaninbabylon #10percentrule #wealthbuilding

## 6. That phone costs 200 hours of your life (*Your Money or Your Life*)
**Hook:** “That ₹1 lakh phone actually costs 200 hours of your life.”
- “Earn ₹80,000 a month for ~160 working hours? Your life energy is worth about ₹500 an hour.”
- On screen: ₹1,00,000 ÷ ₹500/hr = **200 hours = 5 work weeks**
- “Not saying don’t buy it. Just ask: is it worth 5 weeks of my life?” → CTA
**Caption:** Price things in hours, not rupees. Some purchases are worth it. Many aren’t. ⏳ #moneyandlife #mindfulspending

## 7. High income ≠ wealthy (*The Millionaire Next Door*)
**Hook:** “Who gets rich first: the ₹3 lakh earner or the ₹1 lakh earner?”
- “Karan earns ₹3 lakh, spends ₹2.8 lakh, invests ₹20,000. Priya earns ₹1 lakh, lives simply, invests ₹40,000.”
- On screen after 10 years: Karan ≈ ₹46 lakh · Priya ≈ **₹92 lakh**
- “Wealth is what you *don’t* spend.” → CTA
**Caption:** A big salary looks rich. A big savings rate *becomes* rich. #millionairenextdoor #wealth #savingsrate

## 8. Spend big on what you love, cut the rest (*I Will Teach You to Be Rich*)
**Hook:** “I spend on travel without guilt. Here’s my trick.”
- “Pick 1–2 things you love. Spend freely there. Then cut ruthlessly on things you don’t care about.”
- “Unused subscriptions, delivery fees, impulse sales: just ₹3,000 a month…”
- On screen: ₹3,000/month × 20 years → **≈ ₹30 lakh**
- “FIREpath’s Leak Hunter finds yours in 2 minutes.” → CTA
**Caption:** Frugal on what doesn’t matter, generous on what does. 🎯 #consciousspending #budgeting #moneytips

## 9. Your emergency fund is a seatbelt (*The Psychology of Money*)
**Hook:** “In 2020, two investors faced the same crash. Only one lost money.”
- “One had 6 months of expenses in the bank and kept every SIP running. The other had to sell at the bottom to pay bills.”
- On screen: **Emergency fund = 6 × monthly expenses**
- “Leave room for error. Your future self will thank you.” → CTA
**Caption:** The emergency fund doesn’t earn much. It protects everything that does. #emergencyfund #moneysafety

## 10. Asset or liability? (*Rich Dad Poor Dad*)
**Hook:** “Your car isn’t an asset. Here’s the 5-second test.”
- “An asset puts money in your pocket. A liability takes money out.”
- “A ₹15,000 car EMI for 5 years takes money out every month: EMI, fuel, insurance.”
- On screen: Same ₹15,000 invested for 5 years, then left alone for 15 → **≈ ₹73 lakh**
- “Buy the car if you need it. Just know what it really costs.” → CTA
**Caption:** Judge purchases by cash flow, not by how they look. #richdadpoordad #assets #carloan
